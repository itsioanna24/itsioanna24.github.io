Collaborative Discussion 1 - Peer Response 1

Discussion Forum

Reflection on this Artefact

This peer response helped me extend my understanding of the governance and accountability aspects of AI in social media, particularly in relation to ethical regulation and public trust. While my original post focused mainly on the economic impact and ethical risks of AI, the peer introduced the role of multi-stakeholder initiatives such as the World Economic Forum’s AI Governance Alliance, which I had not previously considered in depth. This highlighted how AI governance is not only managed internally by companies but also influenced by broader international collaborations.
I also found the emphasis on customer trust particularly valuable. The reference to IBM research helped me realise that ethical AI adoption is strongly shaped by user perception, not just technical compliance or regulation. This adds another important dimension to the discussion, linking ethical practice directly to business sustainability and reputation.
Overall, this response encouraged me to think more critically about how ethical frameworks are developed and enforced in practice. It strengthened my awareness that responsible AI use depends on a combination of regulation, industry collaboration, and public trust, rather than technology alone.

Collaborative Discussion 1 - Peer Response 2

Discussion Forum

Reflection on this Artefact

This peer response reinforced several key points from my original discussion while also challenging me to think more critically about the practical implications of AI adoption. I found the comparison between early social media platforms and current AI-driven systems particularly useful, as it highlighted the rapid evolution of targeted advertising and how deeply embedded AI has become in shaping user experience and profitability.
I also appreciated the extension of my point regarding organisational knowledge barriers. This difference in perspective helped me think more critically about the balance between technological progress and individual rights.
Overall, this response deepened my awareness of AI’s societal impact, particularly around ethics, education, and digital literacy.

Collaborative Discussion 1 - Peer Response 3

Discussion Forum

Reflection on this Artefact

This peer response helped me broaden my perspective by connecting AI-driven personalisation in social media to its potential application in other critical sectors such as healthcare. While my original post focused on the economic value and ethical risks of AI in social media marketing, this feedback introduced a valuable interdisciplinary comparison that I had not previously considered.
The reference to AI-powered patient engagement tools in healthcare highlighted how similar technologies can deliver measurable improvements in real-world outcomes, such as treatment adherence. This made me realise that the principles behind personalisation at scale are not limited to marketing but can also contribute to societal benefits in areas like health, provided they are carefully governed.
At the same time, the question about whether Meta’s personalisation strategies could be ethically adapted for healthcare prompted me to think more critically about context-specific risks. In particular, it reinforced the importance of stricter privacy frameworks such as GDPR and HIPAA when dealing with sensitive personal data, where the consequences of misuse are significantly higher than in advertising.
Overall, this response encouraged me to think more comparatively and ethically across industries, strengthening my ability to evaluate both the transferability and limitations of AI systems in different domains.
