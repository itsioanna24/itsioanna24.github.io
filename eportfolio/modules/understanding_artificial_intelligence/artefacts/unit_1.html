Reflection on this Artefact

This discussion post helped me deepen my understanding of how AI is not only a technological tool but also a major economic and strategic driver within social media platforms. In researching the topic, I developed a clearer perspective on how AI supports targeted advertising, business growth, and efficiency, particularly for SMEs. I was surprised by the scale of economic impact reported in Meta’s case, which highlighted the real-world significance of AI beyond theoretical applications.
At the same time, this task encouraged me to critically evaluate the limitations and risks of AI adoption. I became more aware of issues such as algorithmic bias, data privacy, and misinformation, which demonstrate that AI systems can produce unintended societal consequences if not properly governed. This balanced perspective was important in moving beyond a purely optimistic view of technology.
Overall, this activity strengthened my ability to integrate academic research into a structured argument while considering both benefits and ethical implications. It also improved my critical thinking skills by requiring me to evaluate conflicting perspectives and reflect on the importance of responsible AI use in digital marketing contexts.
